Can the fresh household RAG lifecycle fail closed and still roll back cleanly?

This click-through models the accepted Open WebUI, Qdrant, Lemonade, secret, sharing, backup, and cutover boundaries from the ownership decision and the corrected prototype question. The household route is one guarded HTTPS origin through Caddy to a Unix socket; native RAG uses Qdrant multitenancy and an exact generation-scoped credential. It uses a public synthetic household and keeps all state in memory. It is a discussion aid, not service evidence or production code; current package files do not override these decision inputs.

Question: does this state model make the fresh initialization, private-by-default document flow, provider readiness, operator-only maintenance, and whole-runtime recovery sequence coherent enough to turn into the disposable service fixture?
